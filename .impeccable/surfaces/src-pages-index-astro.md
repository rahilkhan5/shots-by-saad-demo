Scope: the whole photographer demo site, one page (`src/pages/index.astro`). Mode: Experience.

Audience: wedding and event photographers judging a demo on a phone; story visitors are couples and families planning mehndi, nikah, barat and walima.
Job: let the photos lead, then turn interest into an enquiry. Action: step through a wedding, see packages, check a date, send an enquiry.
Proof: big real wedding photos (credited stock), packages with PKR prices, a working date check and enquiry form.
Constraints: everything labelled demo, photos credited, no real numbers, enquiry and date check simulated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The photographer sketches your wedding day for you, like a friend on a napkin: marker notes, arrows and highlighter around big taped-in photos. It refuses the dark full-screen slideshow with a thin serif name.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper (#fff8e8), black marker ink (#111), highlighters with jobs: yellow for mehndi, pink for barat, teal for walima. Marker caps lettering (Shantell Sans) over a clean sans body (Figtree). Photos are taped on with tape strips, boxes have sketchy double outlines, a doodled photographer mascot guides.

STORY: A couple sees the work large at once, steps through a wedding function by function, reads prices written down, checks their date, and sends an enquiry. A photographer sees their own work leading.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, marker headline with one phrase in pink highlighter and a teal swoosh under it, one line on who and where, a yellow "Check your date" button. Right (below the headline on phones), one big taped barat photo with the mascot pointing its camera at it and a drawn arrow and note.

FORM: Hand-drawn zine, the challenger the user chose, seed key 0b694037. Signature interaction: marker arrows and circles draw themselves on as you scroll, and the date check's mascot changes face for free, one team left, and booked.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
