# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static pages, fast on cheap phones), small client scripts only where the demo needs them, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Same stack as the other demo sites.

## Users

- **Real audience: wedding and event photographers in Pakistan** whom the software house shows this demo to, usually on a phone over WhatsApp. They judge whether their own work would look this good on a site like it.
- **Story audience: couples and families planning a wedding** (mehndi, barat, walima) or an event, comparing photographers on their phones, often sent the link by a relative.

## Product Purpose

A demonstration portfolio website for a fictional wedding and events photographer, built by the software house to show what a photographer's site from them looks like and does. Success: a photographer asks for one like it.

## Positioning

A portfolio that lets the photos lead and turns interest into an enquiry: browse whole weddings by event, see packages with prices in PKR, check whether a date is free, and send an enquiry, instead of an Instagram link and "DM for rates".

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp shares. Pakistani wedding conventions: several functions (mehndi, barat, walima, nikah), wedding season in winter, packages priced in PKR with an advance to book the date, delivery of an album and edited photos weeks later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything is a demo: the photographer, clients, packages, prices and reviews are made up and labelled as a demo where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- Photos come from free stock libraries (Unsplash licence) and are credited; they are not presented as the fictional photographer's real clients.
- No real phone or WhatsApp number. The enquiry and date check show what would happen on a real site instead of sending anything.
- English, with room for an Urdu version on a real build.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photographer, clients or reviews. Photos are free stock images; everything else is authored demo content.

## Product Principles

1. The photos lead: the interface steps back so the work is the first and last thing a visitor sees.
2. Prices in the open: packages and what they include are listed, because "DM for rates" loses enquiries.
3. Honest demo: never let a visitor believe the photographer or the weddings are real.
4. Phone first: most visitors browse on a phone with one thumb.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Real alt text for every photo, readable type over images, works on slow connections (sized images, lazy loading).
